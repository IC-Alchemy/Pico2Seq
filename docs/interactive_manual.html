Tap saves the full session to flash (transport pauses ~0.5s, OLED shows SAVED). Long-press (≥0.4s) reloads last saved state (LOADED).

🛑 2. Auto-Save on Stop

When stopping the transport, after ~1 second of idle time, dirty edits are automatically persisted to non-volatile flash memory.

🛡️ 3. Watchdog Self-Recovery

If the hardware experiences an RP2350 watchdog freeze, the bootloader validates the snapshot CRC32 and recovers the live session automatically.

§ 1 Panel & Hardware Layout

Pico2Seq operates on a dual-surface architecture:

  • 32 Touch Pads (MPR121 @ 0x5A): 4 rows × 8 columns, arranged as Bank 0 (Low Voice of pair) and Bank 1 (High Voice of pair).
  • 8×4 RGB LED Matrix (GPIO 1 FastLED): Direct 1:1 mirror of the step grid with 10 themes, playheads, and polyrhythmic overlays.
  • SliderModule: 4 continuous faders (12-bit ADC) and direct Voice 1–4 selector buttons with Shift chords.
  • ButtonModule8 & Mode Strap (GP7): Dual sets for Param (Note..Octave, Slide, Shift) and Utility (Play/Stop, Save/Load, Scale, Swing, Theme, Enc Target, Randomize, Shift).
  • TMAG5273 Magnetic Encoder (I2C 0x35): Velocity-sensitive 3D hall-effect dial with per-voice base editing.
  • VL53L1X Distance Sensor (I2C 0x29): 55–700 mm hands-free recording with automatic pause outside the window.

§ 2 Quick Start Tutorial

  1. Connect Audio: 48 kHz stereo I2S output on 3.5mm jack. Power via USB-C. Transport boots running at 90 BPM.
  2. Touch Pads: Tap pads in Low Bank (steps 1–16) or High Bank (steps 17–32) to toggle gates.
  3. Shape Parameters: In Param mode, Faders 1–4 modulate Filter, Attack, Decay, and Velocity.
  4. Save / Load: In Utility mode, tap Button 2 to save the session to flash. Long-press (≥0.4s) to reload.
  5. Voice Editing: Hold Shift + V4 to enter deep Voice Editing mode.

§ 4 The 29 Presets & Sound Buffet

Pico2Seq contains 29 built-in presets organized into a 2-page browser (Page 1: 1–24 on pads 8–31; Page 2: 25–29 on pads 8–12). Pads 6 & 7 toggle between pages (`6< >7`).

⚠️ Hardware & Firmware Gotchas Reference

Critical behavioral nuances verified directly against current C++ source.

📏 Lidar Window & Pause on Out-of-Range

The VL53L1X distance window is 55–700 mm. When your hand moves above 740 mm or is removed, recording automatically pauses so steps keep their existing values rather than collapsing to zero velocity/filter!

🎛️ Encoder Edits Per-Voice Base or Selected Step

Turning the encoder edits the selected voice's base parameter, or the step in Step Edit mode. The OLED displays Base for 1.5s after turning. Live edits update the voice in-place without restarting notes.

💾 Session Persistence to Flash

Utility Button 2 saves (tap) and reloads (hold ≥0.4s) sessions. Auto-save triggers ~1s after stopping. The 64-byte frame header is protected with CRC32 integrity checks.

🔇 USB MIDI Removed & Audio Focus

USB MIDI was removed in favor of dedicated 48 kHz stereo I2S audio. The USB-C connector serves as CDC serial console and power only.

⚡ SRAM Hot Audio Execution

All real-time synthesis code runs from RP2350 internal SRAM with zero flash wait states, ensuring completely glitch-free 48 kHz audio buffers.

🎚️ Fader Mode Snapping

Flipping GP7 between PARAM and UTIL causes the physical fader positions to snap to the newly assigned parameters on the first read.
